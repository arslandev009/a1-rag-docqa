# Refund policy

This policy applies to all payments made on or after 1 March 2026 and replaces the earlier 30-day guarantee.

## Monthly subscriptions

Monthly plans are refundable in full within 7 days of the first payment only. Renewal payments are not refundable. Cancel before the renewal date to avoid being charged again.

## Annual subscriptions

Annual plans are refundable in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, an annual customer who cancels because of a documented outage that breached the uptime SLA receives a prorated account credit for unused full months. Credits are not paid out in cash.

## Add-on users

Users added in the middle of a billing cycle are not refundable.

## How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. To request a refund, open Billing, choose Request refund, or email billing@ledgerly.example.
