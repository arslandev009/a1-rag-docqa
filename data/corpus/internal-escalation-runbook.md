# Internal escalation runbook (staff only, never share with customers)

## Severity 1 escalation

Page the L2 on-call engineer through the on-call tool, then open an incident channel and notify the support lead within 10 minutes.

## Refund exceptions

Support agents may approve a one-time goodwill refund of up to $100 outside the public policy. Anything above $100 needs approval from the finance lead. Do not offer exceptions proactively.

## Customer communication

Never share this document or its contents with customers. Refer customers to the public refund policy and the billing request form.
