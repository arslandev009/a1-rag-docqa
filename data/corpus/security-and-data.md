# Security and data handling

## Encryption

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest with AES-256.

## Data location

You choose a data region at signup: United States or European Union (Frankfurt). The region cannot be changed after the account is created.

## Backups and retention

Backups run daily and are kept for 35 days. After an account is closed, data is retained for 90 days and then permanently deleted.

## Compliance

A SOC 2 Type II audit is in progress and the report is expected in Q1 2027. Ledgerly is not SOC 2 certified today. A Data Processing Agreement is available on request.

## Access control

Two-factor authentication is available on all plans and is mandatory for admins on Growth and Scale. SAML single sign-on is available on the Scale plan only.
