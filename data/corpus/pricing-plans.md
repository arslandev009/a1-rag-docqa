# Plans and pricing

Ledgerly is invoicing and payments software for small businesses. All prices are in USD per month, excluding taxes. Prices can also be shown in EUR or GBP at checkout.

## Plans

- **Starter, $19/month:** 1 user, up to 50 invoices per month, 1 integration. No API access, no webhooks.
- **Growth, $49/month:** 5 users, up to 500 invoices per month, 3 integrations, recurring invoices, API access and webhooks.
- **Scale, $129/month:** 25 users, unlimited invoices, unlimited integrations, priority support, SAML single sign-on.

## Extra users

Growth accounts can add users for $6 per user per month, up to 15 users in total. Scale accounts can add users for $4 per user per month.

## Billing options

Annual billing gives two months free: you pay for 10 months and use 12. Plan downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period, never mid-cycle.

## Free trial

Starter and Growth include a 14-day free trial and no credit card is required. The Scale plan trial is available on request through sales.
