# Refund policy (archived)

This archived policy applied to payments made before 1 March 2026.

## 30-day money-back guarantee

All plans, monthly and annual, came with a 30-day money-back guarantee. Customers could request a full refund within 30 days of any payment.

## Processing time

Refunds were processed within 14 days of approval.
