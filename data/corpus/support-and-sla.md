# Support hours and SLA

## Support channels

- **Starter:** email only. First response within 2 business days.
- **Growth:** email and chat, Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 20:00 UTC. First response within 8 business hours.
- **Scale:** email, chat and phone. Severity 1 issues get a first response within 1 hour, around the clock. Severity 2 within 4 hours. Severity 3 within 1 business day.

## Uptime

Scale guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime. If uptime falls below 99.9% but stays at or above 99.0%, the service credit is 10% of that month's fee. Below 99.0%, the credit is 25%. Credits must be claimed within 30 days. Growth has a 99.5% uptime target with no credits. Starter has no uptime commitment.
